Site 120 · Wave 7 · how it was made

ISLET

An isometric island you raise and lower with clicks

01 The idea

A tiny world-builder. Click to raise land, shift-click or use dig mode to lower it; sand, grass, forest, rock and snow follow the height you give.

02 How it works

  • A 22×22 height map from value noise with a radial falloff.
  • Tiles are drawn back to front as a top diamond plus two shaded side faces.
  • Picking checks tiles front to back for the first top face containing the pointer.
  • Heights ease toward their targets, so edits rise smoothly.

03 Type & color

  • Sky blue and coral with a rounded display face.
  • Three fixed brightness levels per block give the 3D read.

04 The screenshot pass

  • A helper named top clashed with the browser's built-in window.top and stopped the script; renamed.

05 Lift this into your site

  • Isometric 3D on a 2D canvas needs only draw order and three shades.
  • Avoid global names that the browser already owns.
