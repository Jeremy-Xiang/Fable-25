BUTTER
the whole trick

Sixty lines. No library.

The content wrapper is position: fixed; a spacer div gives the document its real height so the native scrollbar still works. Every frame: current += (target − current) × lerp, then one transform. Parallax falls out for free — each ornament just multiplies the same scroll value by its own data-speed.

Keyboard & a11y safe

Space, PgDn, arrows, and anchor links all work — the page listens to real document scroll, not hijacked wheel events.

Reduced motion

prefers-reduced-motion (and touch devices) get untouched native scrolling. Smoothing is a garnish, never a gate.

When to use lenis

This is the concept. In production, lenis adds nested-scroll handling, touch inertia, and years of edge cases — the guide says so plainly.

feel it

Parallax is just multiplication.

The dark orb above drifts against the scroll at −0.22×; the outlined one earlier moves with it at 0.15×. No scroll listeners on the elements, no IntersectionObserver — one number each, applied in the same loop that already knows where the page is.
