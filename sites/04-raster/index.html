H1
Three Principles

01

Clarity precedes beauty.

If the reader notices the typography before the message, the typographer has failed upward. Every measurement on this page is a multiple of 8px — check with the grid toggle above.

02

The grid is generous.

Constraint is not the enemy of expression; it is the price of rhythm. Twelve columns yield 4,095 usable combinations. We have used nine of them here.

03

Red is a neutral.

In Swiss hands, #E30613 carries no emotion. It is a structural element, like a beam. This page contains exactly one emotion and it is alignment.
