Wing II finale · site 50 of 50

Two bodies, one line — and for a few seconds, the difference stops mattering.

A stone the size of a thumbnail, held at arm's length, can black out a star ninety-three million miles away. What you're looking at is that geometry, live: a real 3D moon, in front of a real light, on a line drawn by your screen.

Drag the moon — or Tab to it and press the arrow keys — to nudge the alignment.
01 — TOTALITY

The corona behind it
was never still.

The shimmer you're seeing through the moon's silhouette is a generated video loop — plasma streamers combed outward by the sun's own magnetic field, the way they only appear for the few minutes the disc fully covers the light. It plays continuously behind the 3D scene, not as decoration but as the reason the occlusion means anything at all.

02 — THE OCCLUSION

Move the moon and the
ring breaks open.

The dark sphere is a real Three.js mesh, lit only at its own limb by a Fresnel rim shader tuned to the corona's gold. Behind it, unmoving, sits the light it's blocking. Drag the sphere — or focus it and use arrow keys — and it un-eclipses at the edge you exposed, then eases itself back on line the moment you let go.

03 — FIFTY

The last of fifty,
built to close the set.

This piece is deliberately the collection's only site to combine all three of Wing II's techniques at once — a generated hero asset, a generated motion loop, and genuine Three.js 3D — in the same frame. How that decision fits the other forty-nine sites is in the guide.
