PLATE X

A generative Rorschach machine: symmetric ink diffuses onto the card live, then interprets you back

01 The idea

Ink walkers with momentum wander the card stamping soft dabs — and every dab is mirrored across the fold, so bilateral symmetry (the thing that makes blots read as moths and pelvises) is guaranteed. Plates form in seconds, never repeat, and the TELL ME WHAT I SEE button offers interpretations exactly as unreliable as yours.

02 How it works

  • ~26 seeded walkers with heading noise and a gentle pull toward the fold; each stamps a radial-gradient dab at both x and W−x.
  • Walkers branch (up to 130) for lobes and tendrils, shrink as they age, and occasionally throw wet splatter pairs.
  • A frame budget ends diffusion so plates finish; NEW PLATE clears and reseeds. Reduced motion fast-forwards to the finished blot.
  • Readings are a two-array combinatoric — subject × predicament — for 100 deadpan diagnoses.

03 Type & color

  • Cormorant Garamond italics for the clinical-poetic captions, Space Mono for plate numbers.
  • Card cream on consulting-room charcoal; ink is ink.

04 The screenshot pass

  • Verified plates read as classic Rorschach (the mirrored-dab approach worked first try — symmetry does all the perceptual work), edges stay inside the card, and the caption cycle lands.

05 Lift this into your site

  • Mirror-stamping is the whole secret: any random mark-making process becomes 'a face' if you reflect it across an axis.
  • The frame budget matters — generative pieces need endings, or they turn to mud.
  • Radial-gradient dabs at 5–10% alpha build ink better than opaque circles ever will.
