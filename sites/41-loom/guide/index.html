LOOM

Every thread remembers its crossing

01 The idea

Wing II leans on Higgsfield-generated imagery as source material rather than pure procedural generation. LOOM asks what happens if the generative layer doesn't just sit next to a photo, but reads from it — a woven grid whose every cell takes its color from an actual pixel of a macro textile photograph, so the "fabric" the canvas draws is genetically the same cloth as the hero shot above it.

02 The craft

  • One generated hero asset (nano_banana_pro): a macro shot of rust/cream/indigo crochet, used both as a framed "swatch" and as the color source for the canvas weave
  • The image is drawn once onto a hidden 48×27 offscreen canvas — one pixel read per weave cell, no re-fetching or GPU shader needed
  • Basket-weave illusion: cells alternate "over" (horizontal bar, brightened) and "under" (vertical bar, darkened) in a checkerboard — that single alternation is the entire cloth read
  • Unraveling is scroll-driven: columns past a threshold that moves with scroll position drift, jitter and fade, like a loose edge pulling free; scrolling back re-tightens it
  • A pointer-proximity push displaces nearby cells outward, so threads visibly part under the cursor anywhere on the page, not just in one hotspot

03 Type & color

Piazzolla (display/body) + Fragment Mono (labels) · walnut bg #1a140f, rust #c1683a, cream #ede1cd, indigo thread #4a5578

04 Reproduce it

  1. Draw the source image into a tiny offscreen canvas sized to your grid (cols × rows) so getImageData gives one averaged color per cell for free
  2. Render each cell as either a horizontal or vertical bar based on (i+j)%2, with a brightness multiplier to fake over/under shading
  3. Drive one "edge" variable from scroll position; cells past it get a per-cell jitter and alpha falloff proportional to distance from the edge
  4. Add a radial pointer-repulsion term to every cell's draw position, independent of the scroll term, so both effects compose

05 Iteration notes

  • Build pass: first draft had the weave canvas as a static background with the photo only in the hero; moved the photo sampling to feed the weave directly so the two didn't read as two different design languages, and cut a fourth text section that repeated the second's point
  • Code-review pass: checked contrast of cream/dim text on the walnut card background at the actual rendered rgba, added a solid fallback fill behind the canvas so text never sits on a transparent/black flash before the image loads, and gated all per-frame jitter/wobble behind prefers-reduced-motion
  • Live-browser pass: served locally and screenshotted at scroll 0 and scroll-max — caught that the swatch photo overlapped the hero heading on narrow-but-not-mobile widths, fixed by hiding the swatch under 760px and confirming the weave itself still carries the imagery at that breakpoint
