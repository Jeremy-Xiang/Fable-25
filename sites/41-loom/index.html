Every thread
remembers
its crossing.

A generative weave, colored live from a macro photograph of real crochet — warp and weft rendered cell by cell, in the exact rust, cream and indigo the yarn was dyed.

↓ scroll to unravel the pattern

01 — the warp

Sixty-four columns hold tension.

Behind this text a canvas grid samples color directly from the hero photograph — one pixel read per cell, forty-eight across and twenty-seven down. Odd cells sit the weft over the warp; even cells sit under it. That single alternation is the whole illusion of cloth.

02 — the unravel

Scroll, and the right edge lets go.

As you move down the page, columns nearest the right margin lose tension first — they drift, jitter and fade, exactly the way a loose thread pulls free from a finished edge. Scroll back up and the weave re-tightens, cell by cell.

03 — part the threads

Move your cursor across the cloth.

Threads near the pointer push apart, the way real weave gives under a fingertip, then settle back once you move on. Try it anywhere behind this card — the effect runs across the full page, not just here.
