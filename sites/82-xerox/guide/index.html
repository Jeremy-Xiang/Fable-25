COPY OF A COPY

A photocopier that degrades beautifully: scan-bar sweep, contrast crush, toner noise, streaks — compounding by generation

01 The idea

Generation loss as an aesthetic. Press COPY: a light bar sweeps the glass and the output tray receives a degraded print — midtones crushed, toner noise, drum streaks, a sloppy-feed skew. USE LAST COPY AS ORIGINAL closes the loop so entropy compounds until the poster dissolves.

02 How it works

  • The original is a procedurally drawn poster (LOST & FOUND, sad-face moon) — regenerable, no assets.
  • degrade() is an ImageData pass: skew+offset via transformed drawImage, then per-pixel contrast crush (midtones die first), ±noise, vertical streak columns, and edge vignette for drum wear.
  • Prints stack in the tray with random rotation; fidelity readout is 0.82^generation.
  • The scan bar is a WAAPI animation gating the print — theater with a purpose.

03 Type & color

  • Archivo Black poster shouting over Courier Prime bureaucracy.
  • Office beige, toner black, one photocopier-green scan light.

04 The screenshot pass

  • Verified the pristine original, tape corners, button affordances; the degradation math was tuned by copying repeatedly and checking gen-5 is degraded-but-legible, gen-10 is abstract.

05 Lift this into your site

  • degrade() is a portable ImageData filter — feed it any canvas; great for 'archival' treatments of real photos.
  • The compounding loop (output becomes input) is the concept worth stealing; one buttonful of entropy.
  • ImageData is expensive: degrade on demand, never per frame.
