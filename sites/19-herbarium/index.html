Every plant on this page is a sentence in a tiny language: F means grow, + and − mean turn, brackets remember where you were. Apply the rule five times and a single letter becomes a fern. Aristid Lindenmayer wrote the grammar in 1968; your browser is the greenhouse. F → FF+[+F−F−F]−[−F+F+F]
