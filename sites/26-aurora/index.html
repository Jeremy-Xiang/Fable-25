Field notes

The backdrop is a single generated painting — nano_banana_pro, one prompt, one frame. It carries all the texture a shader can't invent on its own: brushy cloud, snow grain, the particular way violet bleeds into teal at a ribbon's edge.

What moves is separate. A value-noise flow field, sampled fresh every frame, pushes a few hundred short-lived strands across the sky in the same three hues as the painting. Turn up the wind and the noise field is sampled faster and rougher — the ribbons thin out and whip instead of drift, the way a real substorm looks when the Kp index climbs.

Teal — OOxygen, ~100–300km, the everyday green-teal curtain
Violet — N₂Nitrogen, lower altitude, appears in stronger storms
Green — O highHigher-altitude oxygen, the rarest of the three here
