How it bends

The photograph never moves — it's the fixed input texture a fragment shader samples every frame. What you're actually seeing shift is the sampling coordinate itself: a pair of slow sine waves nudge each pixel's lookup position by a few thousandths of the canvas, the same way real ice bends light passing through its uneven crystal structure.

On top of that, a second pass layers a classic caustic pattern — overlapping absolute-value sine waves, the cheapest way to fake the shifting light-net you see on a pool floor — tinted glacier-blue and added back into the refracted image. The slider scales both effects together, so "refraction" is one real shader uniform, not a filter.
