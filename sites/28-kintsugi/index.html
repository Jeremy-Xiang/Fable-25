Firing crack repaired with urushi lacquer dusted in gold — the standard kintsugi method, rendered here as an SVG seam instead of an actual repair.

Fracture0% sealed

Why gold

Kintsugi doesn't hide a break, it gilds it — the philosophy being that an object's history, including its damage, is part of what makes it worth keeping. A repaired bowl is often considered more valuable than an unbroken one from the same kiln.

Why animate it here

A static gold line photographs well but reads as decoration. Watching the seam draw itself, once, the moment the piece scrolls into frame, borrows the feeling of the repair actually happening — controlled entirely by stroke-dashoffset, no video, six SVG paths.
