SUMI

Ink finds a moment and holds it still

01 The idea

Wing II's asset pipeline is a Higgsfield generation stage feeding hand-written HTML/CSS/JS — no frameworks, no build step. SUMI pairs that with a genuine video-first hero: a short, looping capture of sumi-e ink dispersing in still water plays behind the page, and a live canvas simulation on top answers back with the same behavior on demand, blooming wherever the cursor moves or clicks. The two ink events — one filmed, one generated — sit in the same visual register so the page reads as a single continuous study rather than a video with an effect bolted on.

02 The craft

  • Hero media is a <video> with a matching poster/fallback <img> pulled from the same Higgsfield generation batch, so a stalled or blocked video never leaves a blank frame
  • An SVG feTurbulence paper-grain overlay sits above the video at mix-blend-mode:multiply, which is what keeps the footage reading as ink-on-rice-paper instead of footage-on-a-screen
  • The interactive layer is a small particle system: each pointer blot eases toward a random max radius, draws as a few concentric rings of falling opacity, then decays — cheap per-frame math that still reads as diffusion because real ink spreads fast-then-slow the same way
  • Click-drops spawn a larger, slower-decaying blot than drag-trails, so deliberate clicks read as a heavier stroke than passive movement
  • Minimal monochrome palette (paper, ink, dim) plus exactly one red seal accent, echoed in a literal seal mark and in the ink-blot fill color, keeps the restraint the brief asked for

03 Type & color

Ma Shan Zheng (brush-feel display) + Inter (body) + Fragment Mono (labels) · paper #f2ede0, ink #17140f, ink-soft #413c32, seal red #b3271c, dim #8b8271

04 Reproduce it

  1. Layer video → paper-grain overlay (multiply blend) → interaction canvas → readability scrim, each as its own fixed full-bleed element stacked by z-index
  2. On pointermove spawn small blots throttled to roughly one every 55ms; on pointerdown/click spawn one larger, slower blot
  3. Each blot eases its radius toward a target with r += (maxR - r) * grow and fades via a per-blot life value decremented once per frame
  4. Draw each blot as 3–5 concentric rings with alpha falling off toward the edge, rather than a single flat circle, to fake soft ink bleed cheaply
  5. Gate autoplay behind a .play().catch() fallback to the still image, and gate all blot decay/spawn behind prefers-reduced-motion

05 Iteration notes

  • Build pass: first draft tried driving the ink canvas off scroll position to mirror LOOM's unravel; dropped that in favor of pure pointer-driven blots since a still-water ink diffusion is inherently a touch/pointer interaction, not a scroll one, and forcing scroll-coupling fought the metaphor
  • Code-review pass: checked ink-soft-on-paper and dim-on-paper contrast at the actual rendered card opacity, confirmed the seal-red accent is used exactly once as a solid mark plus once as the blot fill (not scattered across more elements than the brief's "one red seal accent" intends), and gated video autoplay, blot spawn, and blot decay separately behind prefers-reduced-motion so a reduced-motion visitor gets a static poster and static ink marks rather than a half-animated page
  • Live-browser pass: served locally and tested drag/click on the interaction layer — caught that rapid clicking could pile up unbounded blots and tank frame rate, fixed with a 140-blot cap that trims oldest first; also confirmed the video poster shows correctly when autoplay is blocked by the browser's media-engagement policy
