01 — the stroke

One drop, filmed once.

The hero behind this page is a short looping video of sumi ink dispersing in still water — no loop trickery, just a five-second capture played on repeat, held soft under a paper-grain overlay so the video reads as ink on rice paper rather than footage on a screen.

02 — the diffusion

The canvas remembers the same physics.

Layered on top is a small particle simulation: every blot spawned by your pointer grows toward a random maximum radius with an eased curve, softens at the edge with concentric rings of falling opacity, then fades over a few seconds — cheap to compute, but it reads as diffusion because real ink bleeds the same way, fast at first and slower as it spreads.

03 — try it

Move the brush.

Drag your cursor anywhere on this page to trail small blots; click to drop a larger bloom. Each one is independent — spawn several to watch them overlap and darken where ink meets ink, the way real washes layer.
