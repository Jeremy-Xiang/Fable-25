Site 100 · Wave 6 · how it was made

ONE HUNDRED

The finale: 99 tiles — one per site — assemble into the number 100, scatter into a constellation, and link everywhere

01 The idea

Site 100 doesn't add a new trick; it is the collection. Ninety-nine dots, each carrying a real site's slug and accent color, spring between two formations: pixel positions sampled from the text '100', and a slow spiral constellation. Hover names a site, click visits it — the finale is a working index disguised as fireworks.

02 How it works

  • The '100' targets come from rasterizing the numeral to a tiny canvas and sampling filled pixels — the same text-to-points trick as the LED wall, reused as choreography.
  • Tiles are spring-integrated toward their targets with velocity damping; the trailing-fade clear (32% alpha) turns settling into comet trails.
  • Hover hit-tests the nearest tile within 22px, shows a tooltip with the real slug, and click navigates to ../slug/.
  • Every accent color is the actual accent of that site — the collection's palette, audited.

03 Type & color

  • Fraunces light italics for the bow; JetBrains Mono for the tooltip.
  • Near-black violet and gold — curtain-call colors.

04 The screenshot pass

  • The pass caught 12 wrong slugs — Wave 3 site names were misremembered, which would have 404'd a tenth of the finale's links; corrected against the real directory listing and re-verified.

05 Lift this into your site

  • Text-sampled particle formations are a reusable big-moment pattern: rasterize any word, sample alpha, spring particles to the points.
  • Making celebration functional (hover = identify, click = go) is the difference between confetti and a finale.
  • If you have a collection, its accent colors are a ready-made palette — use the real ones.
