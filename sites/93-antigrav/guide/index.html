ZERO-G

A headline off its leash: every letter is a physics body, and your cursor is a gravity well

01 The idea

GRAVITY IS A SUGGESTION — each glyph drifts in zero-g with mass, momentum and spin. The cursor pulls (or repels) with inverse-square force, clicking detonates a local impulse, walls bounce softly, and RE-TYPESET springs every letter home to its ghost slot.

02 How it works

  • Bodies are canvas-drawn glyphs with mass from measured width; the well applies F = k/d² clamped so nothing slingshots.
  • Home slots are measured by laying out the headline centered — ghost rectangles show where letters belong, a quiet inventory of the chaos.
  • Re-typeset switches to spring+damp toward home with angle unwinding; any pointer move releases them again.
  • Letters over speed threshold flash mint — a cheap, readable energy indicator.

03 Type & color

  • Archivo Black — masses need mass; Space Grotesk elsewhere.
  • Void navy with mint interaction color; red well when repelling.

04 The screenshot pass

  • Verified tumbling states, ghost slots, deck states and the well ring; tuned drag (0.995) and wall restitution (0.7) so the system settles without dying.

05 Lift this into your site

  • The letter-physics pattern needs: measured home layout, per-glyph bodies, one force function. ~120 lines all-in.
  • Ship it with the re-typeset spring — chaos without a way home is a toy, with one it's a design.
  • The impulse click doubles as a natural 'shuffle' affordance for hero text.
