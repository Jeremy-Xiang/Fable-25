MONOLITH Works Studio Guide Index
Spatial Design Studio — Kyoto / Oslo

We build
silence
you can enter.

The object turning behind this text is our only manifesto. Scroll, and walk around it.

I — MATERIAL

Nothing precious, everything considered.

Basalt, brass, pressed air. We choose materials the way composers choose rests — for what they refuse to say. The monolith's skin is a single shader: metal remembering it was once light.

II — GRAVITY

Weight is a rumor we decline to confirm.

Every piece we make floats a few millimeters above expectation. The dust in this room drifts on real physics — slow Brownian arcs, indifferent to deadlines.

III — LIGHT

It reflects a room
that does not exist.

The environment mapped onto the stone is procedural — a cathedral of gradients computed each frame. No photographs were harmed.
