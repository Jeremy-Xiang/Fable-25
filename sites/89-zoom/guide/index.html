Site 89 · Wave 6 · how it was made

POWERS OF TEN-ISH

An infinite zoom: scroll dives galaxy → city → room → atom → galaxy, seamlessly looping

01 The idea

The Eames film as a scroll mechanic. Four procedural scenes exist simultaneously at exponentially-related scales; scroll is a continuous zoom exponent, each scene fading in while small and out as it engulfs the screen. The atom's nucleus is the next galaxy — the loop closes on itself.

02 How it works

  • Each scene is a draw function taking radius + alpha; per frame, each computes rel = wrap(i − zoom) and renders at 9^−rel scale.
  • Fade-in under 60px and fade-out past ~2.4 viewport diagonals keep exactly two scenes visible through any transition.
  • Zoom is lerped from scroll (0.07/frame) so wheel steps become a continuous dolly; the readout shows the compound magnification.
  • Deterministic pseudo-randoms keep galaxy stars and city windows stable across frames.

03 Type & color

  • Spectral's scientific-humanist voice with Space Mono instrumentation.
  • Deep space blue; each scene keeps the shared blue/gold discipline so crossfades feel continuous.

04 The screenshot pass

  • Verified the rest state reads as 'inside the atom, nucleus glowing' after swapping the flat nucleus disc for a radial glow; checked scene handoffs at multiple scroll depths for pop-in.

05 Lift this into your site

  • The wrap-and-scale loop is the machine — swap in your own scenes (they only need draw(r, alpha)).
  • Scale factor 9 with 1.6 rel-culling gives clean two-scene crossfades; change both together.
  • Make the deepest scene contain the first and you get the infinity for free.
