A wax-sealed letter that unfolds panel by panel as you scroll — real CSS 3D creases with fold-angle shading

01 The idea

One idea taken seriously: a four-panel accordion letter, sealed in wax, that unfolds through real rotateX creases as you scroll — and refolds if you scroll back. Shading on each panel follows its fold angle, the way paper darkens as it tilts from the lamp.

02 How it works

  • Panels nest under a perspective stage; each unfolds through its own scroll band (with overlap) from 180° to 0°, alternating fold direction — valley, mountain, valley.
  • A shade div per panel opacity-maps to (1−k)^1.6: cheap, believable Lambert.
  • The letter translates upward as panels open so the composition stays centered; the wax seal lives on the container with translateZ so it rides above the folded stack, breaking at 4% scroll.
  • Backsides are real elements (rotateX(180°) + backface-visibility) carrying handwritten address lines.

03 Type & color

  • Cormorant Garamond for the letter, Caveat for handwriting, Space Mono wayfinding.
  • Desk-green darkness around lamplit paper; one wax red.

04 The screenshot pass

  • The closed state initially read as a blank slab — the seal was buried under folded panels and the outside had no writing. Moved the seal to the container with translateZ and inked addresses on the backsides; re-captured to a proper sealed letter.

05 Lift this into your site

  • The accordion math (per-panel scroll bands + alternating fold sign) generalizes to route transitions and menu reveals.
  • The fold-shading trick — one overlay div, opacity from fold angle — is 3 lines and sells all the depth.
  • Anything fixed relative to folding content needs translateZ above the stack, or it vanishes into the fold.
