Site 96 · Wave 6 · how it was made

LOUD

The brutalist marquee wall: eight belts of giant type at different speeds and directions, hover to hold, scroll to feed

01 The idea

Fashion-week shouting: eight full-bleed rows of Archivo Black scroll forever at staggered speeds and alternating directions. Hovering a belt slows it to 12% (readable, grabbable); clicking reverses it; flinging the page pours your scroll velocity into every belt at once.

02 How it works

  • One rAF loop owns all belts: x -= speed·dir·hoverMul·(1+boost), wrapped at half the track width (content is duplicated 8×).
  • Belt widths are measured after fonts.ready — measuring before web fonts land is the classic marquee bug.
  • Scroll boost is smoothed |Δscroll| so a fling surges the whole wall then decays.
  • Outlined words (-webkit-text-stroke) and two interrupt colors (acid, red) break the rhythm.

03 Type & color

  • Archivo Black exclusively — the wall is the typography.
  • Ink black, acid yellow #ede40e, alarm red; nothing else allowed.

04 The screenshot pass

  • Verified belt seams are invisible at the wrap point, hover-hold works per-row, and the acid rows keep contrast; checked fonts.ready measurement prevents overlap gaps.

05 Lift this into your site

  • The single-loop multi-belt engine replaces N CSS marquee hacks and gives you velocity input for free.
  • Always duplicate content ≥2× the viewport and wrap at scrollWidth/2; measure after fonts.
  • Hover-slow (not hover-stop) is the difference between grabbable and broken.
