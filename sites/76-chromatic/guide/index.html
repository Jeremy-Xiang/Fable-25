CHROMATIC

A WebGL film-strip gallery where scroll velocity drives chromatic aberration and radial blur

01 The idea

The award-site trick where flinging the page smears the imagery like light through a cheap lens. Six procedural “photographs” sit on a vertical strip; scroll position crossfades between neighbors and scroll velocity — smoothed with a lerp — feeds two shader effects at once: RGB channels sampled at radially-diverging offsets (aberration) and a 7-tap radial blur along the ray from screen center.

02 How it works

  • Slides are canvas drawings uploaded as WebGL textures — zero image files.
  • The fragment shader takes texA/texB + mixv for the crossfade and vel for distortion; aberration grows with distance from center so the middle stays readable.
  • Velocity is |Δscroll| per frame, clamped and eased at 0.08/frame so effects swell and settle rather than twitch.
  • A HUD reports frame, velocity, and aberration in px — the page explains itself while you use it.

03 Type & color

  • Unbounded for the wordmark (wide, techy), Space Mono for HUD copy.
  • Near-black violet base #070608; the red/cyan pair in the favicon mirrors the aberration itself.

04 The screenshot pass

  • Verified zero console errors and correct first-slide rendering; captions and HUD confirmed against slide index; velocity effects code-reviewed (they need live scrolling, which stills can't show).

05 Lift this into your site

  • Copy the shader + the frame() loop; swap SLIDES for your images (any drawImage-able source).
  • Keep the velocity clamp (min(1, v/60)) — unclamped trackpads will blind people.
  • The 640vh scroll track is the whole scroll mechanic: one div, no scroll hijack.
