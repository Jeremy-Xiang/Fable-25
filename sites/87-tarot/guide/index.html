The developer's tarot: 3D card flips, pointer-following holographic foil, and readings for The Merge Conflict, reversed

01 The idea

A three-card spread (past/present/future) drawn from a deck of engineering arcana — The Refactor, The Heisenbug, The Rubber Duck — each with an upright and a reversed reading. The cards flip in 3D and carry a holo-foil layer that chases your pointer like a foil trading card.

02 How it works

  • Card flip is the canonical preserve-3d + backface-visibility pattern; flipped cards also tilt ±10° with the pointer for the in-hand feel.
  • The foil is a ::before radial gradient positioned by --mx/--my custom properties, blended with color-dodge — gold/violet/teal interference that moves against the tilt.
  • Reversed cards (40% chance) rotate their art and swap to the reversed reading; DRAW AGAIN reshuffles.
  • Card art is inline stroked SVG, two colors, deliberately woodcut.

03 Type & color

  • Cormorant Garamond for the occult voice; JetBrains Mono for card numbers.
  • Midnight violet, gold + lilac foil — candlelit, not neon.

04 The screenshot pass

  • The pass caught a real crash: two apostrophes were double-escaped in the readings array, terminating the string and killing the whole script — page rendered header-only. Fixed and re-verified clean.

05 Lift this into your site

  • The foil recipe (custom-property gradient + color-dodge) upgrades any card UI in ~10 lines of CSS.
  • Flip + pointer-tilt compose only if the tilt transform includes the flip's rotateY — transforms replace, not add.
  • Write your own arcana; the mechanic is content-agnostic.
