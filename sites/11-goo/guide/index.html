GOO LAB

Tactile happiness research

01 The idea

A fictional lab that studies squish. SVG goo-filter blobs merge and split like lava-lamp specimens, buttons physically squash when pressed, and the whole palette is lab-slime green on clinical white.

02 The craft

  • SVG filter goo (feGaussianBlur + feColorMatrix contrast trick) on animated blobs
  • Squash-and-stretch micro-interactions via WAAPI spring-like easings
  • Blobby section dividers drawn with animated SVG paths
  • Baloo 2 rounded type to match the physics

03 Type & color

Baloo 2 · clinical white, slime green #7bd94a, grape accents

04 Reproduce it

  1. Wrap blob <circle>s in a <g filter="url(#goo)">; animate cx/cy slowly
  2. The goo filter: blur 8–12px, then feColorMatrix alpha 18 -7
  3. On press, animate scale(1.25,.75)→scale(.9,1.1)→1 with bouncy cubic-bezier
  4. Keep blobs behind a z-index seam so text never sits on a merging edge
