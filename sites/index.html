Fable 25 — an exhibition, two wings · built end-to-end by Claude (Fable 5)

Fifty websites, no two alike, every pixel written by a model.

Wing I (01–25): shaders, CSS 3D, generative gardens, playable synths, self-drafting architecture, ASCII engines — zero binary assets, everything procedural. Wing II (26–50): Higgsfield-generated imagery, animated video loops, and real Three.js 3D as the counterpart flex. No frameworks, no build step outside that. Each site ships with a how-it-was-made guide and survived at least three fine-toothed iteration passes in a real browser.

The catalog

01 — 50 · every room is open

Wave 3 — the screenshot wave

Twelve sites, 51–62, each checked against real browser screenshots before sign-off: Mercury (liquid-metal pool), Marionette (string physics), Salvo (fireworks), Censer (shape-holding smoke), Automaton (mechanical songbird), Gloaming (draggable dusk sky), Drape (hand-rolled cloth), Revenant (point-cloud figure), Lathe (throw a vessel), Drift (self-making ambient music), Observatory (live sky dashboard), and Zenith (the finale). Thumbnails are the actual screenshots.

Wave 4 — the liftable wave

Thirteen demos, 63–75 — each a single effect or genre you can copy into a real site, with a live customize panel and a guide that shows exactly what to lift: Splash (fluid cursor trail), Flowmenu (flowing nav), Curveloop (text on a looping curve), Magnetic (buttons that pull toward the cursor), Tilt (3D tilting cards), Decode (scramble-in headlines), Morph (shape-shifting cursor), Shatter (particle text), Reel (drag carousel with momentum), and Ripple (WebGL hover ripple).

Wave 6 — the road to one hundred

Twenty-five sites, 76–100, researched from the current trend wave — velocity-driven WebGL, motion-as-brand, variable-font physics, raymarched 3D — plus the classics the collection still owed itself: a split-flap board, a photocopier, a living topo map, an OS that never shipped, a CMYK press, a Rorschach machine, thirty years of web design on one page, and a finale where all ninety-nine earlier sites take the bow.

Wave 7 — simulations, instruments and toys

Sites 101–125. Things you can play with: physics and math simulations, small instruments, drawing toys, and a reel that sends you to a random site.
