issue 01 — the velocity edition

Type that
feels your scroll.

Every headline on this page sits on a variable font with live wght and SOFT axes. Scroll gently: nothing. Fling the page: the letters gain weight, soften, and physically stretch with the motion — then spring back when you stop. The type is the speedometer.

chapter two

Momentum is
a typeface.

The mechanics: scroll velocity is smoothed with a spring, then mapped onto three channels at once — font-variation-settings for weight (340 → 820) and softness (0 → 80), plus a subtle scaleY squash-and-stretch. Because the axes are real font data, the letterforms re-draw — this isn't a CSS scale faking it.

chapter three

Stop scrolling.
Watch it settle.

The spring is deliberately underdamped — when you release, the weight rings past its resting value once before settling, like a plucked string. prefers-reduced-motion pins every axis to its resting book weight.
