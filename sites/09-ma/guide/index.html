Site 09 of 25 · how it was made

間 MA

A study in negative space

01 The idea

A meditation on the Japanese concept of ma — the meaningful pause. The page is mostly empty on purpose; one vermilion circle travels the scroll like a brush mark, and vertical text sets the rhythm. The restraint is the showcase.

02 The craft

  • writing-mode: vertical-rl typography with Noto Serif JP
  • A single scroll-choreographed element (the enso dot) across the whole page
  • Whitespace ratios held to a 1:1.618 progression between sections
  • Ink-wash texture made from layered CSS gradients, no images

03 Type & color

Noto Serif JP 200 · washi off-white, sumi ink, vermilion #b8433a

04 Reproduce it

  1. Decide what to leave out first; write the cut list before the HTML
  2. Set section padding from a golden-ratio scale, never round numbers
  3. Move one element continuously through the scroll to bind the page
  4. Use vertical-rl for headings only; body text stays horizontal for reading
