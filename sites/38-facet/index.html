01 — The cut

Low-poly on purpose.

The gem is an icosahedron with zero subdivision, flat-shaded so every triangle stays a distinct facet instead of smoothing into a sphere. Fewer faces, sharper light — the opposite instinct from most 3D work, and the right one for a gemstone.

02 — The refraction

Light bends,
on a budget.

A physical material with transmission and a tuned index of refraction fakes the way light passes through and bounces inside real glass — sampling a procedural environment map instead of the room around it, since there is no room, only a shader.

03 — The sweep

One spotlight,
five moods.

A single spotlight orbits the gem on a slow ellipse, cycling through amethyst, sapphire, emerald, topaz, and ruby. Watch one facet at a time — it changes color five times a lap without a single texture ever changing.
