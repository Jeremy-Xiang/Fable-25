TERRAZZO

A tiled macro photo of cast terrazzo, and a scatter of procedural stone chips floating above it — colors picked live from the image itself. Scroll to see them drift, move your cursor to nudge them, or just shake the jar.

Material study

Real terrazzo is cement cast around broken stone, marble, or glass, then ground flat and polished — every chip is a genuine accident, no two floors alike. The generated hero image is one macro shot of a candy-bright version of that: cream cement, chips in four hot colors.

The chips floating above it are not from the photo — they're drawn fresh every load, irregular little polygons in colors sampled directly from real pixels in the image, so the palette always matches even though the shapes never repeat. Scroll and the layers drift at different speeds; move your cursor and nearby chips lean away, like they're floating in something faintly viscous.
