Rules of the playground

One: only the primaries, plus black and the color of good paper. Kandinsky insisted the circle is blue, the triangle yellow, the square red; we honor the palette and ignore the assignments, as every student did.

Two: the grid exists so the diagonal can betray it. Every shuffle places one element at 15 degrees for exactly this purpose.

Three: nothing here is an image. Every form is an SVG primitive, dragged by the same hand that once held the compass — yours.
