The Fabrics

Every textile in Collection № 9 is woven from CSS gradients — conic charcoal, repeating pinstripe, radial oxblood. No photograph could be licensed, so none was needed.

The Reveal

Each look arrives behind a veil of pure black, lifted by clip-path as you approach. In couture as in code, the unveiling is half the garment.

The Grain

The film grain over this page is an animated SVG turbulence tile — nine frames of noise, looping like a projector. It costs 0.4 kilobytes and one apology to photorealism.
